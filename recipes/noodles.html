Wok Noodles with Chicken and Vegetables

Back

About this recipe:

Rice noodles tossed with juicy chicken and vibrant vegetables, all brought together with soy sauce, curry and ginger. A fragrant, satisfying dish with a warmth that keeps you coming back for more.

Ingredients:

  • Rice noodles
  • 2 carrots
  • 1/2 onion
  • 1 green pepper
  • 3 chicken breast fillets
  • Soy sauce
  • Chicken stock
  • Curry
  • Fresh coriander
  • Ground ginger

Steps:

  1. Cook the noodles in a pot of boiling water for 4 minutes. Drain and set aside.

  2. Cut the vegetables and chicken into small pieces.

  3. Heat a drizzle of oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot and a splash of chicken stock. Cook for 5 minutes.

  4. Once the onion and carrot are tender, add the green pepper and cook for a further 5 minutes.

  5. Add the chicken and season with curry, ground ginger and coriander. Add a little more stock if needed.

  6. Once the chicken is cooked through, add the soy sauce and stir well.

  7. Finally, add the noodles and toss everything together before serving.
