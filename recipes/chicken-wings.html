Crispy Baked Chicken Wings with Sweet Potato Fries

Back

About this recipe:

Juicy spiced chicken wings and crispy sweet potato sticks, all roasted together in the oven for an easy, flavour-packed meal.

Ingredients:

  • chicken wings
  • 1 sweet potato
  • olive oil
  • salt
  • black pepper
  • sweet paprika
  • ground cumin
  • garlic powder
  • dried oregano

Steps:

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C with fan and top and bottom heat for 10 minutes.

  2. Meanwhile, cut the sweet potato into sticks. Season with salt, pepper, paprika, garlic powder, oregano and a drizzle of olive oil, to taste.

  3. Season the chicken wings on both sides with salt, pepper, cumin, a pinch of paprika and a drizzle of olive oil.

  4. Place the wings on a baking tray and bake for 5 minutes.

  5. Remove from the oven, turn the wings over and add the sweet potato sticks to the tray. Bake for a further 18 minutes.

  6. Check that the wings are golden and the fries are crispy on the outside and tender inside. If needed, continue baking in 5-minute intervals until done.
